# v1.2.0 — 2026-09-07

Minor release. Previous: [v1.1.0](v1.1.0.md) · Next: [v1.2.1](v1.2.1.md) · Diff: `git log v1.1.0..v1.2.0`

## Features

- Scheduling list with clinic scope and identity search (#14, #15). `GET /meets` gains:
  - filters by `clinic`, participant `first_name`, `last_name`, `national_id` and `mobile`,
    and `participant_uuid` for one user's appointment history;
  - `page`/`page_size` with a `total` count, and `sort_by`/`sort_dir` over a fixed
    allow-list of columns (default `created_at DESC`);
  - keyset pagination as an alternative: `use_cursor` and `cursor` in, `next_cursor` and
    `has_more` out.
  The organizer may be a clinic rather than a person. Super admins see every organizer.
- New `Meet` fields:
  - `settings`: admin-workflow state (`approved_at`, `is_absent`, `present_at`) and booking
    rules. Fields the service doesn't read are opaque and owned by the caller (ravanhealth).
  - `created_at` (read-only) and `creator_uuid` (set once on create, never updated).
  - `version`, an optimistic-concurrency token. `Update` with a stale version fails with
    `ABORTED` instead of overwriting a concurrent write.
- Booking rules enforced on create and update: `min_hours_between_bookings` and
  `prevent_multiple_upcoming_bookings`.
- Availability templates: weekly recurring slots per organizer (weekday, time range, price,
  effective date range), managed at `/meets/availability-templates`. Overlapping templates
  are rejected, and each occurrence is materialized into a meet.
- `AUTH_DISABLED=true` skips the auth service and treats every request as a fully
  privileged local caller. For local, non-public instances only.

## Breaking changes

- `meets` loses its auto-increment `id`; `uuid` (now v7, time-ordered) is the primary key.
- `Meet` fields 12–16 (`first_name`, `last_name`, `national_code`, `mobile`,
  `clinic_name`) are reserved.
