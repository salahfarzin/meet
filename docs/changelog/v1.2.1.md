## Fixes

- Editing or deleting an availability template left its already-materialized future meets
  in place (#16, #17). They kept showing in `GetAvailability`, blocked conflict checks, and
  were never rebuilt from the new schedule. Now the template's unbooked future meets are
  purged in the same transaction as the edit or delete. An edit only purges when a field
  that shapes the meets changed (weekday, times, price, effective dates, active).
